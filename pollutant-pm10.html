Know Your Pollutants / PM10
Particles ≤ 10 µm

PM10
Coarse particulate matter

Dust you can often see and taste. Larger than PM2.5, it mostly lodges in the nose, throat and upper airways.

What is it?

PM10 includes everything up to 10 micrometres: road dust, construction debris, soil, pollen, mould spores and tyre or brake wear. It settles faster than PM2.5 but is constantly re-suspended by traffic and wind.

Where does it come from?

  • Road dust lifted by vehicles
  • Construction and demolition
  • Bare soil, unpaved roads and wind-blown dust
  • Tyre, brake and clutch wear
  • Pollen and mould spores

Health effects

  • Irritates eyes, nose and throat
  • Triggers coughing and worsens asthma and allergies
  • Long-term exposure is linked to reduced lung function
  • Often carries allergens and toxic metals on its surface

In Pune

Road dust (63 of 73) and construction dust (50 of 73) were the top two exposures reported by Pune respondents, and eye and throat irritation were the leading symptoms, typical of coarse-dust exposure.

Reduce your exposure

  • Cover your nose and mouth near construction sites
  • Keep windows closed during dusty hours
  • Wet-mop rather than dry-sweep indoors
  • Support dust control: water spraying and covered construction

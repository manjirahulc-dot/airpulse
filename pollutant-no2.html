Know Your Pollutants / NO₂
Reddish-brown gas

NO₂
Nitrogen dioxide

A sharp-smelling gas formed whenever fuel burns at high temperature. It is the clearest chemical fingerprint of traffic.

What is it?

Nitrogen dioxide is one of the nitrogen oxides (NOx). Engines and furnaces produce nitric oxide (NO), which quickly converts to NO₂ in air. NO₂ is also a key ingredient for forming ozone and secondary PM2.5.

Where does it come from?

  • Petrol and especially diesel vehicles
  • Power plants and industrial boilers
  • Generators and gas cooking
  • Open burning

Health effects

  • Inflames the airways and reduces lung function
  • Increases asthma symptoms and respiratory infections, especially in children
  • Concentrations are highest within about 100 m of busy roads
  • Contributes to acid rain and smog

In Pune

Our survey found 38 of 73 respondents live or work within roughly 100 m of a major road, the zone where NO₂ concentrations are typically highest.

Reduce your exposure

  • Choose quieter back-streets for walking and cycling
  • Keep engines off while waiting
  • Ventilate gas-cooking areas with an exhaust fan
  • Maintain and service vehicles regularly
